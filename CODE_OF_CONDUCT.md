# Code of conduct

FM Starship is a small project made by people who like the same game. Everyone should feel welcome here, whatever their experience, and
whoever they are.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to this.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume the worst.
- Take feedback on your work well, and give it in a way that helps.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Discrimination of any kind, including over someone's race, gender, sexuality, disability, age, religion or nationality
- Sexual language or images
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Spam, or anything else that makes this a worse place to be

Football rivalries are fine as long as they stay about football.

## This applies to

Issues, pull requests, Discussions and anywhere else you're speaking for the project.

## Reporting a problem

Use **Report content** in the `...` menu on the post, or report the account from its profile. To tell a maintainer privately, open a
[private report](https://github.com/FMStarship/FMStarship/security/advisories/new) and say that it's about conduct.

Maintainers will read every report and keep it private. They may edit or remove content, and warn or ban anyone who breaks these rules.
